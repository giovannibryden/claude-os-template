# Decision Log

Record of major decisions, the reasoning behind them, and outcomes. Critical for learning from your choices.

## Template for New Decisions

```markdown
### [Decision Name] - [Date]

**Context:**
[What led to this decision]

**Decision:**
[What you decided to do]

**Reasoning:**
1. [Reason 1]
2. [Reason 2]
3. [Reason 3]

**Alternatives Considered:**
- [Option 1] - [Why rejected]
- [Option 2] - [Why rejected]

**Key Tradeoffs:**
[What you're giving up with this choice]

**Expected Outcome:**
[What you expect to happen]

**Success Metrics:**
[How you'll know if this was the right call]

**Review Date:** [When to assess outcome]

**Actual Outcome:** [To be filled in later]

**Lessons Learned:** [To be filled in later]
```

## Decision Categories

Tag your decisions for easier reference:

- **Hiring** - New roles, candidate selections, offers
- **Org Structure** - Team reorganizations, reporting changes
- **Strategy** - Direction changes, priority shifts
- **Process** - New workflows, tool adoptions
- **Budget** - Spending decisions, resource allocation
- **People** - Promotions, PIPs, role changes
- **Product** - Technical direction, architecture choices
- **Partnerships** - Vendor selections, collaborations
