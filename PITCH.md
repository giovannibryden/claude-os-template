# Why Use Claude OS?

## The Problem You're Solving

You're managing a team, juggling deals, hiring, strategy work, and constant context switching. Your knowledge is scattered:
- 1:1 notes in random docs
- Candidate feedback buried in email threads
- Meeting insights that never surface again
- Strategy decisions you revisit because you forgot the reasoning
- Action items across multiple systems

You make decisions with incomplete context because pulling together all the relevant information takes too long.

## What This System Does

Claude OS is a personal operating system that captures everything you know and makes it instantly retrievable. Claude becomes your strategic thought partner with full context about your team, deals, candidates, and decisions.

### Real Examples

**Before a 1:1:**
```
You: "What should I discuss with Sarah today?"

Claude: *Pulls last 3 1:1 notes, identifies open action items,
        surfaces pattern from team-themes about her skill development,
        reminds you about the promotion discussion from 2 weeks ago*
```

**After a candidate interview:**
```
You: *Drop transcript in inbox*

Claude: *Evaluates against your hiring rubric, flags concerns about
        technical depth, compares to other candidates for the role,
        drafts your hiring committee recommendation*
```

**Drafting team communication:**
```
You: "Draft an email to the team about the Q2 strategy shift"

Claude: *References your quarterly plan, recent 1:1 themes showing
        team concerns, writes in your voice, anticipates questions*
```

**Pattern recognition:**
```
Claude: "I'm seeing the same concern about unclear priorities come up
        in 4 different 1:1s this week. Worth addressing in the team meeting?"
```

## Time Investment vs. Payoff

**Setup:** 20 minutes
**Daily use:** 0 extra time (you're already taking notes)
**Payoff:** Hours saved per week on context switching, meeting prep, decision-making

The system works because you're already generating this information—meeting notes, candidate feedback, strategy docs. You're just capturing it in one place where Claude can synthesize it.

## How It Works

1. **Capture:** Drop information into the system (meeting notes, transcripts, thoughts)
2. **Organize:** Claude files it in the right place automatically
3. **Synthesize:** Claude connects information across sources
4. **Retrieve:** Ask Claude anything, get answers with full context

The structure is simple:
- `/team` - Your people (1:1s, performance, themes)
- `/hiring` - Candidates and open roles
- `/deals` - Active opportunities you're working
- `/strategy` - Long-term plans and decisions
- `/inbox` - Drop anything here, Claude processes it

## Try It

1. Clone this repo
2. Run `./SETUP.sh` (5 minutes)
3. Drop your next meeting transcript in `inbox/`
4. Tell Claude "process inbox"
5. See what happens

## Who This Is For

This works best if you:
- Manage a team (any size)
- Have recurring 1:1s you want to track
- Make hiring decisions
- Need to synthesize information across multiple sources
- Want a thought partner who remembers everything

## What You're Not Getting

This isn't:
- A project management tool (use your existing PM software)
- A CRM for tracking customers (you have one already)
- A shared team wiki (this is personal, not collaborative)
- Magic (you still need to capture information, Claude just organizes and synthesizes it)
