# Team Update Template

Use this for regular team communications about changes, wins, or important updates.

---

**Subject:** [Team Name] Update - [Brief Topic]

Team,

[Opening: Why you're sending this / context]

**[Section 1 Header - e.g., "What's Changing"]**

[Details about the change, decision, or update]

**[Section 2 Header - e.g., "Why This Matters"]**

[Impact, reasoning, benefits]

**[Section 3 Header - e.g., "What This Means for You"]**

[Specific implications for team members]

**[Optional Section: "Next Steps" or "Timeline"]**

- [Action or milestone 1]
- [Action or milestone 2]
- [Action or milestone 3]

[Optional: FAQ section if anticipating questions]

[Closing: Open door for questions, expression of confidence, appreciation, etc.]

[Your Name]

---

## Common Use Cases

### Announcing a Strategic Change
- New priority or focus area
- Process changes
- Reorganization

### Celebrating a Win
- Deal closed
- Product launched
- Team recognition

### Providing Transparency
- Company update that affects team
- Explaining a decision
- Sharing progress on goals

### Setting Expectations
- New initiative
- Policy change
- Upcoming event or deadline

## Tone Tips

- **Be direct** - Lead with the main point
- **Be transparent** - Share the why, not just the what
- **Be specific** - Avoid vague corporate speak
- **Be human** - Write like you talk
- **Anticipate questions** - Address them proactively
