# New Hire Announcement Template

Use this when announcing a new team member.

---

**Subject:** Welcome [Name] to the [Team Name] team!

Team,

I'm excited to announce that [First Name] [Last Name] is joining us as [Title] on [Start Date].

[First Name] comes to us from [Previous Company] where they [brief description of relevant experience]. They bring [X years] of experience in [relevant area] and will be [what they'll be working on / who they'll support / which region they'll cover].

[Optional personal touch: fun fact, location, team they'll join, who they'll work closely with]

Please join me in welcoming [First Name] to the team. [His/Her/Their] first day is [Date], and [he/she/they] will be [reporting to / working with] [Manager/Team].

[Optional: logistics like where they're based, how to reach them, when they'll meet the broader team]

Looking forward to having you on board, [First Name]!

[Your Name]

---

## Variables to Fill In

- [Name] - Full name
- [Team Name] - Your team name
- [Title] - Their job title
- [Start Date] - When they're starting
- [First Name] - First name only
- [Last Name] - Last name only
- [Previous Company] - Where they came from
- [X years] - Years of experience
- [relevant area] - Their domain expertise
- [what they'll be working on] - Their initial focus
- [Date] - First day
- [Manager/Team] - Who they report to or work with
- [Your Name] - Your signature
