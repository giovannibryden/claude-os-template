# Feedback Request Template

Use this when asking for input on a decision, direction, or initiative.

---

**Subject:** Input needed: [Topic]

[Team / Person Name],

I'm working on [what you're deciding / planning] and want your input before moving forward.

**Context:**
[Brief background on the situation or decision]

**The Question:**
[Specific question you need answered or feedback you're seeking]

**Options I'm Considering:**
1. [Option 1] - [Brief pro/con]
2. [Option 2] - [Brief pro/con]
3. [Option 3 or "Something else entirely"]

**What Would Help:**
[Specific type of feedback you need - experience, concerns, preferences, suggestions, etc.]

**Timeline:**
[When you need feedback by and why]

[Optional: How to provide feedback - async in doc, meeting, Slack thread, etc.]

Thanks for your input on this. [Optional: why their perspective matters]

[Your Name]

---

## Tips for Good Feedback Requests

1. **Be specific** - "What do you think?" is too broad. Ask precise questions.
2. **Provide context** - Share enough background for informed input
3. **Set constraints** - Give a deadline and preferred feedback format
4. **Make it easy** - Offer specific options or questions to react to
5. **Explain why** - Help people understand why their input matters

## When to Use This

- Before making a significant decision
- When you want team buy-in
- When others have relevant expertise
- When the decision affects others
- When you're genuinely unsure and need perspectives
