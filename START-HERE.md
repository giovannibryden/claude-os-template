# Start Here

Welcome to your personal operating system.

## First Steps

1. **Read SETUP.md** - Make sure you've completed the basic setup
2. **Read CLAUDE.md** - This explains the system structure
3. **Fill in your org-chart** - Start with what you know: `team/org-chart.md`
4. **Set your priorities** - Document what you're focused on now: `personal/priorities.md`

## Quick Wins

### The Easiest Way to Start: Use the Inbox
Drop any file—meeting notes, transcripts, email threads, whatever—into `inbox/` and tell Claude "process inbox." Claude will read everything, file it where it belongs, and tell you what it found. Zero thinking required.

### Before your next 1:1
Copy `team/1-1s/template.md` and rename it for your team member (e.g., `sarah-2026-02-13.md`). Fill in what you want to cover. After the meeting, dump your notes in there and tell Claude about it.

### After your next candidate interview
Copy `hiring/candidates/template.md`, rename it with the candidate's name, and fill in your notes. Ask Claude "Should we move forward with [name]?"

### When you need to draft a message
Check `communications/templates/` for starting points. Or just ask Claude to draft it and it'll use your voice.

## Daily Pattern

1. **Morning**: Ask Claude "What's on my plate today?" or "What should I be thinking about?"
2. **After meetings**: Drop transcripts or notes into `inbox/` and let Claude organize them (or put them directly in the right folder if you know where they go)
3. **End of day**: Quick brain dump to Claude of anything that happened, or drop a notes file in the inbox

## The Key Insight

This system is only as useful as the information you put into it. But you don't need to organize that information—that's Claude's job. Just dump it in and Claude will:
- File it where it belongs
- Connect it to other relevant information
- Surface patterns and risks
- Help you make decisions when you need them

You're building a second brain. Feed it, and it'll serve you.

## Examples of What to Ask Claude

**Before meetings:**
- "Pull up my notes on Sarah"
- "What should I discuss with my team today?"
- "Prep me for the 1:1 with Marcus"

**After meetings:**
- "Process inbox" (after dropping transcript/notes)
- "What are the key themes from this week's 1:1s?"
- "Update my action items based on this conversation"

**For decisions:**
- "Should we move forward with this candidate?"
- "What's the pattern I'm seeing across the team on morale?"
- "Help me decide between these two approaches for [project]"

**For communications:**
- "Draft an email to the team about [topic]"
- "Write a Slack message announcing [news]"
- "Help me give feedback to [person] about [situation]"

## Week One Goals

Don't try to fill everything out. Just:
- [ ] Document your team (org-chart.md)
- [ ] Set your current priorities (personal/priorities.md)
- [ ] Create 1-1 files for your next 2-3 meetings
- [ ] Drop one meeting transcript in inbox and process it
- [ ] Ask Claude to help with one real decision

Build the habit first. The system will grow naturally from there.
